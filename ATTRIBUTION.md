# Attribution and source-history audit

Audited on 2026-10-09 (America/New_York), beginning with this project's round-eleven `53f0385` baseline and completed with the five-stage incorporation below. This records the sources of our work, distinguishing the earlier Jain witnesses, the selected five-stage theorem and related work. It is a contribution audit, not a new mathematical review of every contributor's results or a worldwide priority determination.

## How to cite this work's predecessors

An opening acknowledgement in the order **OpenAI, Douglas (Doug) Colkitt, Rohan Arun, Swapnil Jain** is appropriate for the research context discussed in this project. It is not a ranking of contributions or a claim that the mathematics passed through those four people in that order. Cite the particular construction, proof, or implementation where it is used, and preserve its predecessors. The authors of an imported network remain its authors even when another researcher integrates or verifies it.

Our initial manuscript explicitly credited Colkitt's independently sized complex network. Jain's round-six announcement and source then supplied the numerical network for our first major update; his rounds ten and eleven supplied the next adopted networks. Rohan's work was part of the community research we followed, and Jain's notices explicitly credit his weighted matching. These relationships are more informative than ordering a list by the latest headline exponent.

## What the history supports

1. **OpenAI supplied the original problems and mathematical framework.** The #109 manuscript supplies the multiplication/network background; the #130 manuscript supplies the exact-complex model, tensor-to-Fourier compiler, supplied-root convention and all-length reduction. The stronger Fourier work continues to use that reduction. Its authorship must not be assigned to our project or to the later network formalizers.
2. **Colkitt contributed research as well as maintaining the community repository.** The earliest reachable commit, [`5cf29ec`](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/commit/5cf29ecd0d1965fe99bca87ed8d56b413244a24e), contains parameter refinements and certificates. Subsequent commits develop routing, shared/paired circuits, compact controls and complex-network compression. His `h_c=25` independent-complex note at `6e56487` is explicitly cited in our [initial manuscript](https://github.com/shea256/fourier-transform-below-nlogn/blob/eb83242/manuscript.md). Later maintainer reviews integrate and distinguish community constructions; they do not make him their sole author.
3. **The early community gains were composite.** icekylinx's whole-residual batching (#10), partial swaps (#18), endpoint gauges (#24) and copied centers (#36) enter the early chain. Rohan Arun's fixed-middle-profile/corner composition is present in [`50e54ec`](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/commit/50e54ece17afa4bd3cccd1927e9cdea5098038c2) and the [PR #39 audit](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/3b6b66891c0ac888521cf591fe306c6286601d4f/docs/research/community-final-audit.md). His #44 matching and #49 order search are credited alongside their inherited producers and recovery machinery, not as inventions of the entire network.
4. **Jain's independent branch is a direct source for our published witnesses.** Our [`bf38c00`](https://github.com/shea256/fourier-transform-below-nlogn/commit/bf38c00) imports round six; [`a2840b1`](https://github.com/shea256/fourier-transform-below-nlogn/commit/a2840b1) adds rounds ten and eleven. His own notices distinguish imported data, mechanisms reimplemented from descriptions, and his selected words, searches, integration and checking. We retain those distinctions.
5. **The larger frame/cover gains have specific authors.** icekylinx's #130 and #144 introduce the cited generalized frame/cover and paired-cube construction; #144 explicitly adopts an664's #128 completed-core sharing principle and eumemic's #117 producer. Jain's later words use eumemic's #168 modules and related frame/reuse work, James Chang's birth and terminal mechanisms, and DaysSky's carrier extensions. Jain's round-eleven notice also credits Chafik's #176 terminal paired cubes and acknowledges his #181 cube-local configuration.
6. **The five-stage Fourier update has additional authors.** Avi Eisenberg rebuilds icekylinx's #184 source-assisted flow on eumemic's v4 modules in #191/#193/#194. Jacob Sussman supplies the five-stage layout, general frame proof in the #130 RAM model, certificate framework and formal Fourier connection. Chafik Boukhalfa supplies the #200/#233 reuse pairing, #256 explicit-program conversion and the stronger Fourier instantiation in [Sussman repository PR #2](https://github.com/jacobalansussman/wht-power-saving-lean/pull/2). These are upstream results incorporated with a reproduced Lean build and exact checks, not new network or formalization claims by our project.

## Corrections and boundaries identified

- Our short attribution understated Colkitt's research by emphasizing maintenance, omitted Rohan's explicitly cited matching precedent, and did not name Chafik's existing round-eleven contribution or Paureel's earlier two-stage role prominently enough.
- The #256 `NOTICE` at `db3f75c` associates `rohanarun` with `PR209/237`. Public PR metadata identifies **#209 as Jacob Sussman's**, while **#237 is Rohan Arun's**. Our own descriptions use those checked identities. Imported notices should remain unmodified, with this clarification alongside them.
- The vendored #233 `NOTICE` retains an earlier description of changed frames and a `7.0861e-4` profile. Its revised `109a857` package instead keeps PR168's frames and changes the pairing, certifying `0.0007099074`. Use that pinned construction's actual certificate and current proof description for technical claims, while preserving the notice's authorship and license information.
- Sussman's related-work file and Chafik's #225 notice acknowledge this project's earlier written Fourier transfer. We report those acknowledgements as their attribution, not as an exhaustive independent priority finding. OpenAI still supplies the general tensor-to-DFT reduction.
- Our contribution is the written conditional transfer and additional local verification of the selected upstream witnesses. AI assistance is disclosed separately; another same-assistant check is not independent human mathematical review. Crediting a person does not imply their endorsement, collaboration, or review of this paper.

Future imports should add the exact source revision and contribution to this record, preserve original notices and licenses, and state whether the work is incorporated, historical, related, or still under evaluation. Do not remove predecessor credit when the selected bound changes.
